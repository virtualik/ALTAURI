# ALTAURI Commercial Licensing

ALTAURI is free software under the **GNU AGPLv3** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
The AGPLv3 permits any use — personal, educational, commercial, SaaS — as long
as you comply with its terms, chiefly: when you modify ALTAURI and serve it
over a network, you must offer your modified source to those users.

## When you do NOT need anything from us

- Personal and educational use
- Open-source projects (AGPLv3-compatible)
- Non-commercial research, student projects
- SaaS / cloud services **that comply with AGPLv3** (modified source offered to network users)

## When a commercial license is the practical path

AGPLv3 is a strong copyleft license. If your business cannot or does not want
to satisfy it — for example embedding ALTAURI into a closed-source product or
a service you are not ready to open — a commercial license removes that
obligation:

- Closed-source proprietary applications
- Commercial IoT products with closed firmware
- Enterprise internal systems that cannot carry AGPL duties
- White-label solutions

## Commercial license benefits

- Legal protection and clarity
- Priority technical support
- Early access to new features
- Integration consulting
- White-label options
